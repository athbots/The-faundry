# IP-001 — Institutional Physics

**Version:** Genesis G-0.1  
**Status:** Frozen  
**Authority:** CONST-001  
**Depends On:** CONST-001

## Purpose

IP-001 defines invariant behavioral laws governing institutional information, authority, causality, capability, memory, and learning.

IP-001 is implementation-independent.

## Normative language

**SHALL** — mandatory.  
**SHALL NOT** — prohibited.  
**SHOULD** — recommended unless justified otherwise.  
**MAY** — permitted.

## Definitions

**Reality:** External state against which institutional claims and actions operate.

**Observation:** Recorded perception of a phenomenon, condition, measurement, or event.

**Evidence:** Observation or collection of Observations evaluated for relevance and reliability against a claim or Inquiry.

**Knowledge:** Institutionally accepted model, understanding, or conclusion supported sufficiently for its intended use.

**Decision:** Authorized selection of an action, constraint, allocation, or direction from available alternatives.

**Capability:** Demonstrated ability to reliably produce a defined outcome under defined conditions.

**Authority:** Formally recognized power to make, approve, modify, or invalidate a specified class of institutional Decisions.

## Laws

### IP-001 — Reality
Validated reality SHALL override institutional belief. Repeated reliable contradiction between reality/evidence and an active institutional claim SHALL trigger review.

### IP-002 — Separation
Observation, Evidence, Knowledge, Decision, and Execution SHALL remain distinguishable.

### IP-003 — Traceable Causality
Material institutional effects SHALL be traceable to relevant causes where such causes are known. Missing historical links SHALL be recorded as missing, never fabricated.

### IP-004 — Identity
Every persistent institutional object SHALL possess one permanent identity independent of representation, revision, owner, or storage.

### IP-005 — Historical Preservation
Institutional history SHALL remain reconstructable. Supersession, deprecation, correction, or archival SHALL NOT silently erase prior reasoning.

### IP-006 — Causal Direction
Causal claims SHALL respect temporal and logical order. This law does not prohibit dependencies on planned or future objects.

### IP-007 — Evidence
Claims SHALL NOT acquire institutional authority solely through repetition, seniority, popularity, or confidence.

### IP-008 — Uncertainty
Material uncertainty SHALL remain explicit. Unknown, unverified, uncertain, false, and impossible SHALL NOT be treated as interchangeable.

### IP-009 — Governance Separation
Evidence informs governance. Governance determines authorized action. Governance SHALL NOT retroactively redefine evidence merely to justify action.

### IP-010 — Capability
Knowledge of how to produce an outcome SHALL NOT be treated as equivalent to demonstrated ability to reliably produce it.

### IP-011 — Capability Degradation
A capability that is no longer reliably reproducible SHALL NOT remain fully active solely because it existed historically.

### IP-012 — Local Optimization
No subsystem SHALL optimize a local metric when doing so materially reduces total institutional capability without explicit authorization.

### IP-013 — Feedback
Execution SHALL produce observations capable of influencing future institutional decisions through an explicit evidence/learning pathway.

Canonical pathway:

Execution → Observation → Evidence → Learning → Decision

### IP-014 — Learning
Validated failure SHALL be eligible to generate institutional learning to the same structural degree as validated success.

### IP-015 — Reversibility
Consequential decisions SHOULD record reversibility, reversal cost, reversal conditions, and reversal evidence.

### IP-016 — Authority
Authority SHALL be explicit and role-based.

### IP-017 — Bounded Authority
Authority SHALL define what may be decided, what may not be decided, under what conditions, for what scope, and subject to what review.

### IP-018 — Conflict
Materially conflicting active objects SHALL be represented as an explicit conflict requiring evaluation of authority, scope, evidence, and applicability.

### IP-019 — Non-Erasure
Correction SHALL preserve historical state and reason for correction.

### IP-020 — Institutional Learning Record
Significant institutional action SHOULD support recording expected outcome, actual outcome, variance, cause, learning, and required change.

### IP-021 — Entropy
Institutional knowledge SHALL be treated as requiring maintenance, evidence, use, or review to retain practical reliability. Age alone neither validates nor invalidates knowledge.

### IP-022 — Proportionality
Governance burden SHOULD be proportional to impact, irreversibility, uncertainty, dependency, and blast radius.

### IP-023 — Institutional Independence
Institutional memory SHOULD NOT depend on a single person, AI model, vendor, software platform, storage provider, or proprietary format.

### IP-024 — Machine Non-Authority
AI output SHALL NOT acquire constitutional or governance authority solely by being generated by an AI system.

### IP-025 — Founder Continuity
The Institution SHALL remain capable of preserving constitutional identity without undocumented founder memory.

### IP-026 — Authority Precedence
Lower-level specifications SHALL NOT silently override higher-authority specifications.

Authority hierarchy:

CONST → IP → FM → Type Specification → Object Instance

### IP-027 — Execution Reality
A specification that repeatedly fails under validated real-world conditions SHALL enter review regardless of internal logical consistency.

## Verification baseline

An implementation SHALL be capable of demonstrating:
- identity preservation;
- historical preservation;
- explicit authority;
- uncertainty representation;
- evidence/governance separation;
- conflict detection;
- capability-state integrity;
- execution feedback;
- AI non-authority;
- constitutional precedence.

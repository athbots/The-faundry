# CONST-001 — Constitution of The Foundry

**Version:** Genesis G-0.1  
**Status:** Frozen  
**Authority:** Constitutional  
**Scope:** Institution-wide

## Preamble

The Foundry exists to preserve and continuously improve the Institution's ability to perceive reality, reason correctly, make decisions, create capability, and transfer capability across generations.

The Foundry is the constitutional operating system of the Institution.

The Foundry is independent of any founder, employee, software system, product, technology, vendor, or artificial intelligence system.

## Article I — Institutional Identity

### CONST-I-001
The Institution exists to create enduring capability and enduring value.

Capability is a primary institutional asset.

Products, services, revenue, and technologies are expressions of capability, not substitutes for institutional purpose.

### CONST-I-002
The Foundry governs institutional reasoning and institutional memory. It does not govern personal opinion.

### CONST-I-003
Institutional knowledge is stewarded by the Institution. Contributors retain authorship; the Institution retains institutional stewardship.

## Article II — Authority

### CONST-II-001
Constitutional authority defines who may govern institutional action. It does not determine factual truth.

### CONST-II-002
Factual correctness is determined through reality and validated evidence, not institutional rank or agreement.

### CONST-II-003
Governance determines authorized action. Governance does not create factual truth.

### CONST-II-004
Execution implements authorized action and generates information about outcomes. Execution does not acquire constitutional authority through performance.

### CONST-II-005
CISA is the guardian of architectural and institutional consistency. CISA is subordinate to this Constitution and SHALL NOT amend constitutional authority unilaterally.

## Article III — Institutional Memory

### CONST-III-001
Institutional reasoning and history SHALL remain reconstructable.

### CONST-III-002
Incorrect, obsolete, superseded, or inconvenient reasoning SHALL NOT be intentionally erased merely to remove its historical existence.

### CONST-III-003
Institutional decisions SHALL preserve, where applicable:
- context;
- assumptions;
- relevant evidence;
- alternatives considered;
- rationale;
- authority;
- outcome.

## Article IV — Institutional Learning

### CONST-IV-001
The Institution SHALL maintain the ability to learn from validated success and validated failure.

### CONST-IV-002
Institutional learning SHALL remain capable of revising institutional knowledge and practice.

## Article V — Institutional Integrity

### CONST-V-001
Persistent institutional objects SHALL be identifiable, traceable, governed, and historically reconstructable.

### CONST-V-002
Lower-level specifications SHALL NOT override constitutional requirements.

## Article VI — Constitutional Stability

### CONST-VI-001
The Constitution SHALL remain independent of implementation technology and storage representation.

### CONST-VI-002
Constitutional amendments SHALL preserve superseded wording, rationale, objections, authority, and effective version.

## Article VII — Constitutional Amendment

Amendment sequence:

Proposal → Impact Analysis → Conflict Analysis → Ratification → Version Increment → Historical Preservation

No amendment may silently rewrite constitutional history.

## Constitutional Invariants

- `CI-001` Institutional identity is not dependent on representation.
- `CI-002` Institutional reasoning is preserved.
- `CI-003` Constitutional authority is explicit.
- `CI-004` Factual claims remain subordinate to validated reality/evidence.
- `CI-005` Capability and enduring value remain central institutional objectives.
- `CI-006` Institutional history remains reconstructable.
- `CI-007` Persistent institutional effects remain traceable.
- `CI-008` Institutional learning remains continuous.

# What The Foundry Is For

- **Status:** Plain-language project-purpose summary; explanatory, not a new rule.
- **Basis:** Repository Charter and Genesis G-0.1 foundation.
- **Reviewed:** 2026-09-27

The Foundry is being built to help a future engineering and scientific organization make important decisions and carry out difficult work without losing the reasons, evidence, authority, or history behind it.

It gives people a shared way to record what they observe, investigate what those observations mean, check claims against evidence, decide who may act, carry out authorized work, verify the result, and preserve what was learned. Later teams should be able to understand what happened, why it happened, what supported the decision, who had authority, and what remains uncertain.

The repository holds the principles, records, specifications, working software, and checks used to develop this way of operating. A file, implementation, or passing test does not by itself create institutional authority or prove production readiness. Production certification requires its own approved authority and evidence.

**In one sentence:** The Foundry aims to help a long-lived engineering institution turn observations into carefully checked understanding, authorized action, and learning it can reconstruct later.

This summary explains the project's intent in plain language. The Repository Charter, foundation, decision records, and approved specifications remain the controlling sources for their respective scopes.
